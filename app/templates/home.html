Privacy

Your files stay with you

Most free PDF websites send your document to a company's computer, change it there, and send it back. Your file ends up somewhere you cannot see, and you have to trust that it is deleted.

RedPDF works differently. The whole tool loads into this page, and your file is opened by the page itself. It is never sent anywhere. That is also why there is no waiting and no daily limit: your own computer does the work, not ours.

This matters most for the documents people usually need these tools for. Passports, contracts, bank statements, medical letters.
