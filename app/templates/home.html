We don’t get your files.

Most free PDF websites send your document to a company’s server, change it there, and send it back. Your files end up somewhere you cannot see, and you have to trust that they are deleted.

RetroPDF works differently. Everything runs inside your browser, on your device. Your file is never sent anywhere. That is also why there is no waiting and no daily limit: your own device does the work, not ours.
