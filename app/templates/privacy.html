Privacy

What we know about you.

Almost nothing, and your files least of all. Here is the whole of it.

Your files.

Your files are never sent to us. Not a copy, not a summary, not the name. Every tool on this site runs inside your own browser, so the file you choose is opened by the page you are looking at and never leaves the device it is already on.

This is not a promise about what we choose to do with your documents. There is no upload address to send them to. The page is not permitted to make network requests at all, which is a rule your browser enforces rather than something we ask you to believe. The security page explains how to check that yourself.

What your browser keeps.

Three things, all stored by your own browser and none of them ever sent anywhere:

  • Whether you have already seen the opening animation, so it does not replay every time you open a page. This is a date and nothing else.
  • Where you had scrolled to, so reloading puts you back. This is a number and nothing else.
  • Whether you have asked for a desktop app, so the button does not count you twice. This is a date and nothing else.

Neither identifies you. Neither is a cookie, so neither is sent to the server with your requests. You can clear both at any time through your browser's settings for this site, and nothing here will break.

What our server sees.

The same thing every web server sees when you open a page: a request for an address, along with your IP address and which browser you are using, because that is how the internet delivers a page to you. We do not log these, and we do not build a record of visits.

We do keep one number: how many people have opened the page about a desktop app. It is a single tally with nothing attached, kept so we know whether building one is worth the effort. It says how many, never who, and that page shows you the number itself.

What we do not do.

  • No accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for and no password to lose.
  • No advertising, and no advertising networks.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No cookies of any kind.
  • No third party scripts. Everything the page loads comes from here.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because there is nothing to sell or share.

Who runs this.

RetroPDF is run by Eduard Rednic, one person in Finland. Not a company, and not paid for by anyone. Under the GDPR that makes him the data controller for whatever little is described above, which is almost nothing. You can write to hello@retropdf.com about anything on this page, and it reaches him directly.

Why we are allowed to.

The GDPR asks anyone handling personal data to say what entitles them to do it. Serving you a page requires your IP address to reach you, and that is a legitimate interest in the plainest sense: without it there is no way to deliver anything you asked for. The count of desktop app interest holds no personal data at all, so nothing in the GDPR applies to it.

We ask for no consent because we do nothing that needs it. There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, which is the usual reason a site puts a banner in front of you.

How long anything is kept.

  • Your files: never held, so there is no period to state.
  • What your browser stores: until you clear it, and it is yours to clear whenever you like.
  • Server request records: not kept. Access logging is switched off deliberately.
  • The desktop app tally: kept indefinitely, being a single number.

Your rights.

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data an organisation holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. We mention this because you are entitled to know it, not because there is much to ask for: we hold no accounts, no addresses and no record of visits, so in almost every case the honest answer is that we hold nothing about you at all.

If you want to ask anyway, write to hello@retropdf.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority if you think something here is wrong. In the EU that is the authority in the country you live in, and the European Data Protection Board keeps a list of them. We would rather you told us first, but the right is yours either way.

Changes.

If this changes, the date below changes with it. If it ever changes in a way that means your files are sent somewhere, that will be said plainly at the top of this page rather than buried in it.
